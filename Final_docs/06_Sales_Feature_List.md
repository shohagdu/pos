# JSA MULTISERVICES POS — Feature List for Sales

A guide for sales staff: what the software does today, and what is coming next.
Use Part 1 when talking to a shop owner. Use Part 2 only as "on our roadmap", never as a promise with a date.

**In one line:** a cloud POS, inventory and accounts system for Bangladeshi shops. It works in **Bangla and English** on a computer, tablet or mobile phone, with no installation.

---

## Part 1 — Available now

### 1. Selling at the counter (POS)
- Fast billing with a **barcode scanner** or by searching the product name (Bangla or English).
- **Keyboard shortcuts** for speed: F2 search, F4 customer, F6 coupon, F7 invoice discount, F8 pay, F9 hold, F10 held bills, and more.
- **Hold and resume bills**: serve the next customer while one is still deciding.
- **Split payment**: cash, card, bKash, Nagad, bank, or several of these on one bill.
- **Partial payment and due (baki)**: the due amount goes on the customer's account automatically.
- Line discounts and invoice discounts (amount or %), with a **discount limit per staff role**.
- Price changes and selling below the minimum price only for staff who are allowed to do so.
- Change amount shown on cash payment.
- Optional product pictures on the POS screen and in search results.
- **Works on mobile**: the POS has a phone layout with Products and Cart tabs and a Pay button at the bottom.

### 2. Receipts and invoices
- **80 mm and 58 mm thermal receipts** and **A4 invoices**.
- Bangla text prints correctly on thermal printers.
- Shop logo, address, phone, BIN, footer text.
- Reprint any time, marked "REPRINT".
- Print with or without returned items.

### 3. Discounts, offers and coupons (new)
- **Automatic offers**: % off or fixed amount off a product, a category, a brand or the whole bill.
- **Buy X Get Y** (for example Buy 1 Get 1, or buy a phone and get a cover free). The POS asks before adding the free item.
- **Flash sales** with a quantity limit, and **scheduled offers** (dates, days of week, happy hours).
- **Coupon codes**: single use or multi use, minimum purchase, per-customer limits.
- Offers for selected branches only.
- The customer always gets the **best offer** automatically.
- Promotion report: how much each offer gave away and how much it sold.

### 4. Products and catalog
- Categories, brands, units, SKUs, multiple barcodes per product.
- **Variants** (size, colour and so on).
- Retail price, wholesale price, minimum selling price, VAT rate.
- Product photos.
- **Warranty** per product (days, months or years); look up a warranty by invoice.
- **Batch and expiry tracking** (oldest expiry sells first), with an expiry report. Useful for pharmacy, grocery and cosmetics.
- **Bulk import from Excel/CSV**, including opening stock.
- **Barcode label printing** on roll labels or A4 sticker sheets. The system can also create barcodes automatically.

### 5. Stock (inventory)
- Live stock per branch and location.
- Stock movement history for every product.
- Stock adjustments with a reason (damage, loss, gift).
- **Stock count** (physical counting); the differences are posted automatically.
- **Stock transfer between branches**.
- Low-stock alerts and reorder levels.
- Stock valuation at average cost.

### 6. Purchases and suppliers
- Purchase bills, with stock added automatically.
- **Purchase orders** with partial receiving.
- Purchase returns to the supplier.
- Supplier payments and supplier due tracking.
- Supplier ledger and statement.

### 7. Customers
- Customer list with phone number; quick-add from the POS.
- **Customer due (baki) tracking** and due collection.
- Customer ledger and statement.
- Opening balance for existing customers.

### 8. Returns and cancellations
- Full or partial sales returns, with refund or adjustment against the customer's due.
- Correct handling of returns from discounted and Buy-X-Get-Y bills.
- Same-day cancellation only for staff who are allowed to cancel, and only with a reason.
- Nothing is ever deleted: every change is kept in history.

### 9. Money, expenses and income
- Cash, bank and mobile-wallet accounts (bKash, Nagad, Rocket).
- Transfers between accounts, and a cash book.
- Expenses and other income, by category.
- **Bank reconciliation**.

### 10. Cash register and shifts
- Open a shift with the opening cash; close it by counting the drawer.
- The system shows expected cash vs counted cash, and the difference.
- Shift report for each cashier.

### 11. Accounting (built in)
- Double-entry accounting runs automatically in the background.
- Chart of accounts and journals.
- **Trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet**, general ledger.
- Period lock: closed months cannot be changed.

### 12. Reports (download as CSV)
- Dashboard with today's sales, profit and due.
- Sales report (daily, by period, product, cashier, branch).
- **Profit report**.
- Purchase, inventory, product movement and expense reports.
- Customer due, supplier due and aging reports.
- Payment report by method.
- **VAT report**.
- Discount report and promotion report.

### 13. Multiple branches and staff
- Several branches under one business, with stock and sales kept per branch.
- **Staff invitations** with ready-made roles (owner, admin, branch manager, cashier, accountant) or your own custom roles.
- Staff can be limited to the branches they work in.
- **Audit log**: who did what, and when.
- Login history and active sessions; sign out other devices.

### 14. Notifications
- In-app notifications (low stock, due reminders and more).
- Each user chooses which notifications they want.

### 15. Safety and reliability
- Cloud based: data is safe even if the shop computer breaks.
- Each business's data is fully separate from every other business.
- No double sale if the internet drops while saving a bill.
- Daily backups; the owner can download a data export.
- Phone-number (OTP) verification and password reset.

### 16. Plans and billing
- Free trial, then monthly, quarterly, annual or **lifetime licence** plans.
- Pay by bKash, Nagad, Rocket or bank transfer, then submit the transaction ID.

### Who it is for
Retail shops, grocery and super shops, fashion and clothing, electronics and mobile shops (warranty), pharmacies and cosmetics (batch and expiry), wholesalers (wholesale price, due), and businesses with several branches.

### Selling points to remember
1. Fully in Bangla, made for Bangladeshi shops (baki, bKash, VAT/BIN).
2. Works on any device: no installation and no expensive hardware.
3. POS, stock, purchases, due, accounts and reports in one system, not five.
4. Offers and coupons, the kind big chain shops use.
5. Owner control: staff permissions, discount limits, audit log and shift cash checks.

## Part 2 — Planned (future development)

Tell customers these are on the roadmap; do not promise dates.

### Near term
- **SMS notifications** to customers: invoice, due reminders, offers.
- **Email** of invoices and reports.
- **Excel (XLSX) and PDF export** of reports.
- **Mushak-6.3** VAT invoice format.
- **QR code** on receipts.
- **Online payment gateway** (bKash/SSLCommerz) for subscription payments.
- **Two-factor login (MFA)** for owners and admins.
- **Multiple units with conversion** (for example buy in cartons, sell in pieces).
- **Customer groups and price levels** (VIP, wholesale customers).
- **Exchange** (return and new sale in one step) and return approval.
- **Recurring expenses** and expense attachments (bill photos).
- **Back-dated sales** for allowed users.
- More than one coupon per bill.

### Mid term
- **Loyalty points** and customer rewards.
- **Gift cards and store credit**.
- **Serial / IMEI tracking** for mobile and electronics shops.
- **Offline POS**: keep selling when the internet is down; sync later.
- **Restaurant POS** with tables and a kitchen display (KDS).
- **Pharmacy module** (generic names, prescriptions, drug compliance).
- **E-commerce sync** (WooCommerce and online store stock).
- **Public API and webhooks** for integrations.

### Long term
- **HR and payroll**.
- **Custom report builder**.
- **White-label** (the business's own brand) and **custom domains**.
- **Dedicated database** for large enterprise customers.
- Native mobile apps (today the web app already works on mobile).
- AI sales forecasting and reorder suggestions.
